Located in Hase Omi-cho, Sakyo-ku, Kyoto, the project site embodies the ideal *feng shui* configuration of "a mountain at the back and water in the front." To the north, a range of hills provides protection and stability, while a river flowing from the southeast to the northwest gathers *yang* energy—a vital, life-affirming force—and distributes it throughout the space. The gently sloping terrain channels sunlight toward the center of the site, further enhancing its warmth and vitality. Lush vegetation nourishes the land’s inherent life energy, and the presence of human activity introduces a vibrant, human-centered energy (*jinki*), creating a dynamic balance between stillness and movement. Set apart from the main roads, this tranquil residential area offers an environment that harmonizes quietude with a sense of lively engagement. Here, the mountains, water, greenery, and people converge to form a continuous cycle of *qi* (vital energy), fostering a harmonious setting that nurtures longevity, prosperity, and overall well-being.
