The "New Health-Oriented City" envisions an urban environment where every design decision is dedicated to a single, ultimate objective: keeping people active. In this city, the spatial factors that lead to prolonged sedentary behavior are eliminated—for instance, homes do not include traditional living rooms. Automobiles are entirely absent; instead, all streets are reserved for walking, cycling, and social interaction. Swimming pools and running tracks are woven into the very fabric of daily life, appearing throughout the city as natural extensions of public space. At ground level, a dynamic mosaic—seamlessly blending forests, recreational facilities, and grocery stores—encourages constant activity and social engagement. Above this, functional structures are elevated (supported by elements such as *pilotis*), with residential units positioned on the uppermost levels; the entire complex is interconnected by a network of footbridges and pedestrian decks. Within this multi-layered structure, residents can enjoy a comfortable and convenient life, relying solely on walking rather than motorized transport. Here, physical movement is not forced, but rather "subtly prompted" through design. Spatial cues—such as gradients, clear sightlines (visual corridors), and open circulation paths—naturally encourage residents to walk, climb, and explore. Furthermore, the city integrates the principles of Chinese *feng shui* with urban ventilation strategies; carefully positioned "guiding walls" control airflow, ensuring that optimal wind speeds are maintained throughout the urban environment. Meanwhile, the vegetation system follows the logic of East Asian medicinal botany, combining plants that produce synergistic therapeutic effects to enhance the well-being of both mind and body. Every neighborhood is designed to welcome sunlight and life energy (*qi*), fostering an atmosphere imbued with vitality, longevity, and happiness. In essence, this city embodies a "realistic utopia"—a living organism that integrates architecture, health, and environmental intelligence to form a comprehensive urban ecosystem, one that supports an active, joyful, and long-lived existence.
